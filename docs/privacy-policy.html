Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2025  ·  Effective: June 2, 2025

🔒 Short version: Purgify does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. All operations happen entirely on your device.

1. Who We Are

Purgify ("the App") is developed and maintained by MetaVision RS ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Anurag Sen. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at ceo@metavisionrs.in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Purgify is a local utility app. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address or any account information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, photos or media content.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Purgify stores the following data only on your device using Android's SharedPreferences (a standard local key-value store):

  • XP and level — your gamification progress
  • Streak counter — how many consecutive days you have cleaned
  • Total cleaned MB — cumulative junk removed
  • Total boost count — number of RAM boosts performed
  • Last clean date — timestamp of most recent clean (used for streak calculation)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Permissions Explained

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Used to scan your external storage for junk files (APK leftovers, log files, temp files, thumbnails in DCIM). Files are only deleted when you explicitly tap the clean button. We do not read your photos, videos or personal documents.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO

Required on Android 13+ to access media directories for thumbnail cache scanning. We only read file sizes and paths — we never read, copy or transmit media content.

KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES

Required to terminate background apps during RAM Boost. This is a standard Android permission that only affects non-foreground processes.

GET_PACKAGE_SIZE

Used to read the cache size of installed apps so we can display accurate "Other Apps Cache" figures in the cleaner results.

BATTERY_STATS / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Battery level is read to display the battery indicator on the home screen. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED is declared but not actively used in this version.

5. Third-Party Services

Purgify uses the following third-party services within the app UI only:

  • LottieFiles CDN — animated icons are loaded from assets2.lottiefiles.com when an internet connection is available. No personal data is sent; only a standard HTTP request for the animation JSON file.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics platforms or advertising networks are integrated.

6. Children's Privacy

Purgify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app contains no in-app purchases, social features or external links targeted at minors.

7. Data Security

Because Purgify stores all data locally on your device and transmits nothing to external servers, the security of your data is governed entirely by your device's built-in security (encryption, lock screen, etc.).

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data (XP, streak, clean history) is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also clear the app's data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Purgify → Clear Data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
